# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy environment:

- Demonstrating real empathy and understanding for other people
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences constructively
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals but for the community

Examples of behavior that is unacceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination in any form (explicit or subtle)
- Sexually intense language or imagery, sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission (doxxing)
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable
behavior and take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any
behavior that they deem contrary to these standards. Community leaders may take
issues regarding a single individual privately.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and when a member is
officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported privately to the community maintainers. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated confidentially, and reporters will be protected from
retaliation.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining
consequences for any action they deem in violation.

### A. Correction

**Impact**: A brief, inappropriate use of community language or other behavior
judged unprofessional in the community.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, clarifying
the nature of the violation and providing an explanation of why the statement
was appropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### B. Warning

**Impact**: A violation through an isolated incident against community standards.

**Consequence**: A formal warning and restrictions on participation in any
community interactions (e.g., posting public messages). Continued violations
may lead to a longer ban.

### C. Temporary Ban

**Impact**: A serious violation of community standards.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban of the named community member from the public
community spaces for a specified period of time.

### D. Permanent Ban

**Impact**: Consistent demonstrating of patterns of violations of community
standards, repeated or with raised severity.

**Consequence**: Permanent ban from any public interaction in the community.
